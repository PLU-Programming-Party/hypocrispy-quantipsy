The phrase, which purposely misspells ""Haci"" of the Spanish word ""Así,"" is often associated with an image macro of Green M&M seated on a couch, and the pairing is used as a reaction image by people sharing embarrassing situations and dealing with an unwavering and over-confident attitude.
